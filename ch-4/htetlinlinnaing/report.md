## Notes (optional)

DevPath AI is designed for junior developers, career switchers, and self-taught
builders who need a clear path from a target role to job-ready evidence. The
app validates its roadmap structure, keeps provider credentials server-side,
uses privacy-conscious request handling, and includes a click-to-load YouTube
demo on the landing experience.
